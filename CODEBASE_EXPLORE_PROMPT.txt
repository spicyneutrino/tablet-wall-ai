## 2. Writing style

Use a writing style inspired by approximately **75% ASD-STE100**.

The goal is clarity, precision, and readability without making the explanation feel like a maintenance manual.

Prefer:

- short and direct sentences,
- clear subject-verb relationships,
- precise technical terminology,
- one main idea per sentence where practical,
- definitions before heavy use of terminology,
- concrete wording instead of vague references,
- explicit references to files, functions, and components.

Avoid:

- unnecessarily long paragraphs,
- overly academic wording,
- vague phrases such as "this" or "that" when the referent is unclear,
- excessive jargon,
- unnecessary metaphor,
- filler,
- repeated explanations,
- overly compressed explanations that assume too much background knowledge.

Do not oversimplify technical details to the point of being incorrect.
